    Chapter recap · Food and nutrients
    NutrientWhat it doesCommon sources
    CarbohydratesSupply energy. Glucose is readily available. Starch must be digested.Fruit and honey contain glucose. Potatoes, rice and pasta contain starch.
    FatsProvide energy and help insulate the body.Butter, cream and oils.
    ProteinsSupport growth and repair.Fish, eggs, beans and lean meat.
    Vitamin CHelps prevent scurvy.Citrus fruit, potatoes and other vegetables.
    Vitamin DHelps prevent rickets.Oily fish and fortified foods. Our skin also makes it in sunlight.
    WaterHelps cells work, regulates temperature and acts as a solvent.Drinks and foods such as vegetables.

    A balanced diet provides nutrients in suitable amounts. A deficiency disease can develop when the body receives too little of a nutrient.
